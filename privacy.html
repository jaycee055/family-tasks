Privacy policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Family Tasks is a free app for sharing daily goals, reminders and tasks inside a private family group. We keep as little information as possible.

What is stored

  • Your account: a username and a scrambled (salted and hashed) form of your password. Your real password is never stored.
  • Your family group: its name, its invite code, each member's nickname and role (admin or member).
  • Tasks: the task text, who it is for, who made it, its date, time, how it repeats, and the days it was marked done.
  • Notifications: short messages such as "Mama assigned you: Buy rice". These are deleted after 14 days.

Where it is stored

All of this is kept in a private Google Sheet in the Google account of the person who runs this copy of the app. It is not sold or shared with anyone, and there are no ads or trackers. Only members of your family group can see your group's tasks. The app also keeps a copy of your group's tasks on your phone so it works offline.

Before the Google Sheet is connected, the app runs in demo mode: everything stays only on your own phone.

What is loaded from the internet

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The font (Poppins) comes from Google Fonts. Your data goes only to the Google Apps Script that runs the Google Sheet.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows reminders and family updates on your phone. Reminders in the Android app are scheduled on the phone itself. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone's settings.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Me → Delete my account. This removes your login and takes you out of your family group. Tasks you created for the family stay with the family until someone deletes them. If the last member leaves a group, the group and all its tasks are deleted.

Children

Families may add children to their group. A parent or guardian should create and manage a child's account. We do not ask for anyone's real name, email, phone number or location.

Contact
